## 5. Decision-Making Process

1. **Propose:** either member raises an option with a brief rationale.
2. **Discuss:** the team weighs the trade-offs together (cost, accuracy, time, data constraints), with advisor input if needed.
3. **Decide:** the team reaches consensus. **Tiebreaker:** because the team has two members, a split decision is taken to Dr. Zhao at the next advisor meeting, and the team follows his recommendation. If a decision cannot wait, the role holder for that area decides (Lead Developer for technical decisions, Coordinator for schedule and process decisions).
4. **Document:** the decision and its reasoning are recorded in the design notebook and the repository.

## 6. Conflict-Resolution Plan

1. **Address it directly:** the two members talk it through privately and promptly.
2. **Bring it to the team meeting:** if unresolved, it goes on the agenda for the next Friday meeting with a proposed solution.
3. **Escalate to the advisor:** if still unresolved, the team asks Dr. Zhao to mediate or clarify direction.
4. **Involve the instructor:** as a last resort, the team contacts the course instructor.

Repeatedly missing the agreed response window or deadlines is handled through this same process, starting at step 1.

## 7. Work Standards

- All project work is committed to the shared GitHub repository, not kept only on a personal machine.
- No real patient data or confidential hospital information is used. Restricted datasets (such as MIMIC-IV) are used only after the required training and under their data use agreements, and are never entered into third-party AI tools.
- AI assistants may be used for brainstorming, drafting, and coding help, but each member is responsible for reviewing and understanding everything they submit, in line with University of Cincinnati academic integrity policy.
