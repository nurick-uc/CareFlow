## Project Sought

I am interested in developing a healthcare-focused software engineering or data science project that addresses practical challenges in hospital operations. I am particularly interested in combining artificial intelligence, machine learning, predictive analytics, and healthcare data to improve operational efficiency and support better resource-planning decisions.

My current Senior Design project, **CareFlow: Hospital Operations and Resource Planning Platform**, focuses on developing a platform that uses historical hospital operational data to forecast patient admissions and bed occupancy, monitor bed availability, and estimate staffing requirements. The goal is to help hospital administrators and healthcare staff anticipate changes in demand and make more informed resource-planning decisions.

**Possible project directions and features:**

- **Hospital Admission Forecasting:** Apply machine learning and historical hospital data to predict patient admissions over a seven-day period, helping administrators anticipate changes in demand.
- **Bed Availability and Occupancy Monitoring:** Analyze hospital occupancy data across departments to identify capacity constraints and potential bed shortages.
- **Staffing Requirement Estimation:** Compare estimated staffing needs with available staffing levels to identify potential shortages and support workforce planning.
- **Healthcare Data Analytics:** Develop data pipelines to collect, validate, process, and analyze hospital operational data for use in forecasting and reporting.
- **Operational Decision Support:** Present predictive insights and resource-planning information to help hospital administrators and healthcare professionals evaluate operational needs.

**Technical focus:** Python-based data processing and machine learning, predictive modeling, data engineering, database management, and full-stack application development. The project will initially use historical and aggregated hospital operational data, with CSV-based data imports and configurable planning assumptions.

**Goals for the project:** Strengthen my applied machine learning, data engineering, and software development skills while combining my computer science background with my previous biomedical research and hospital experience. I hope to develop a practical, portfolio-ready application that demonstrates my ability to build an end-to-end technical solution for a real-world healthcare problem.
